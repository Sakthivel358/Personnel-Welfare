I. Non-Clinical Boundaries & Welfare Mission

Clause 1: Non-Clinical Decision-Support Scope

WelfareAI is engineered solely to compute observational stress strain indices from self-reported operational factors. It does not perform psychiatric diagnostics, prescribe pharmaceutical treatment, or substitute for formal medical evaluation by qualified military medical officers.

Clause 2: Absolute Non-Punitive & Non-Disciplinary Doctrine

Check-in scores and AI attribution signals are protected by an inviolable non-punitive doctrine. An ML prediction must never automatically become a disciplinary action. Data collected in this portal shall under no circumstances be entered into Annual Confidential Reports (ACR), court-martial inquiries, promotion screening, or disciplinary reviews.

Clause 3: Voluntary Participation & Self-Determination

Personnel engagement with self-checkins is voluntary, respectful of personal autonomy, and focused on self-awareness and proactive recovery.

II. Identity Separation, Pseudonymization & Privacy Protection (Task 36)

Clause 4: Separation of Identity from Welfare Analytics

Direct identity attributes (Name, military service number, contact details) are strictly isolated from analytical telemetry databases. Analytical pipelines operate exclusively on cryptographic user tokens (e.g. USR_7F29A) and generalized demographic buckets.

Clause 5: Data Minimization & K-Anonymity Bucketing

Exact biological age is coarsened into 5-year cohorts (e.g. 25–30) and granular troop postings are generalized into regional tactical units (e.g. Sector-IV Brigade Delta). Telemetry ingests strip all non-essential attributes.

Clause 6: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

Access is strictly compartmentalized across isolated roles: (a) Personnel: private access to personal records; (b) Welfare Officers: view triage flags and follow-up schedules without command authority; (c) System Admins: infrastructure health and audit oversight with zero access to private questionnaires.

Clause 7: Appropriate Masking Policies

Operational rosters mask military service IDs (e.g. CRPF*****52), telephone numbers (******3210), and conceal personal names in default triage views to prevent subconscious appraisal bias.
