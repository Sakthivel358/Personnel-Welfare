Privacy, Ethics & AI Governance Policy

Ethical AI Framework, Data Privacy & Governance Charter

Official 26-Clause Ethical Governance, Data Security, and Operational Privacy Charter for WelfareAI.

CRITICAL ETHICAL DIRECTIVE: The system is a WELFARE SUPPORT and DECISION-SUPPORT platform for uniformed forces. It is strictly NOT a medical diagnosis system and must NOT diagnose psychiatric illness. It must NOT be used for promotion, demotion, termination, disciplinary action, performance reviews, or clandestine surveillance.

I. Non-Clinical Boundaries & Welfare Mission

Clause 1: Non-Clinical Decision-Support Scope

WelfareAI is engineered solely to compute observational stress strain indices from self-reported operational factors. It does not perform psychiatric diagnostics, prescribe pharmaceutical treatment, or substitute for formal medical evaluation by qualified military medical officers.

Clause 2: Absolute Non-Punitive Doctrine

Check-in scores and AI attribution signals are protected by an inviolable non-punitive doctrine. Data collected in this portal shall under no circumstances be entered into Annual Confidential Reports (ACR), court-martial inquiries, promotion screening, or disciplinary reviews.

Clause 3: Voluntary Participation & Self-Determination

Personnel engagement with self-checkins is voluntary, respectful of personal autonomy, and focused on self-awareness and proactive recovery.

II. Data Isolation, Cryptography & Access Governance

Clause 4: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

Access is strictly compartmentalized across three isolated roles: (a) Personnel: private access to personal records; (b) Welfare Officers: view triage flags and follow-up schedules without command authority; (c) System Admins: infrastructure health and audit oversight with zero access to private questionnaires.

Clause 5: Zero Clandestine Surveillance Policy

The platform contains zero clandestine background tracking, GPS surveillance, keystroke logging, audio snooping, or biometric surveillance. Only explicitly submitted form entries are processed.

Clause 6: Data Minimization & Protected Fields

Only variables directly correlated with operational strain (duty hours, recovery sleep, work pressure rating, PSS score) are collected. No irrelevant personal or commercial telemetry is gathered.

Clause 7: Cryptographic Password Hashing

All credentials are hashed using Bcrypt with 10 salt rounds before database insertion. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged in any format.

Clause 8: Secure Signed Session Tokens

User sessions are authenticated via cryptographically signed JWT tokens transmitted through secure, HttpOnly cookies with Strict SameSite enforcement to prevent CSRF and XSS token theft.

Clause 9: Immutable Audit Logging

All security-sensitive operations (logins, password modifications, officer alert reviews, follow-up completions) are written to an append-only, tamper-evident audit ledger with IP and timestamp metadata.

III. Explainable AI, Fairness & Human-in-the-Loop

Clause 10: Algorithmic Transparency & Anti-Black Box Guarantee

Every prediction is accompanied by a transparent mathematical feature attribution breakdown derived from Random Forest tree attributions, showing exact operational contributors.

Clause 11: Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard (No Automated Actions)

The AI model cannot autonomously trigger administrative actions, duty transfers, or roster removals. All interventions require human Welfare Officer assessment and mutual consultation.

Clause 12: Model Fairness & Bias Auditing

The Random Forest architecture uses balanced class weights and regularized hyperparameter bounds to prevent demographic or rank-based statistical bias.

Clause 13: Personnel Right to Challenge or Clarify Scores

Personnel have the unqualified right to submit contextual notes with check-ins or request direct in-person review with the Welfare Officer to clarify operational anomalies.

IV. Operational Pacing, Edge Security & Compliance

Clause 14: Rest Rotation & Pacing Recommendations

Guidance generated by the system is supportive and non-coercive, offering sleep hygiene strategies, sensory reset pacing, and peer buddy connection.

Clause 15: Family & Dependent Data Safeguards

Family assistance requests are routed exclusively to the dedicated Dependent Support Cell and are strictly isolated from operational duty rosters.

Clause 16: Welfare Officer Confidentiality Oath

Designated Unit Welfare Officers operate under a strict code of ethical confidentiality similar to ombudsman privileges within armed forces frameworks.

Clause 17: Right to Profile Correction

Personnel may update their non-operational profile preferences (contact information, emergency details, sleep baseline preferences) at any time in the Profile portal.

Clause 18: Data Retention & Anonymization

Longitudinal check-ins are retained during active deployment and automatically de-identified upon personnel transfer to prevent cross-battalion profiling.

Clause 19: Air-Gapped / Edge Readiness

The platform is designed to run entirely on sovereign, air-gapped local intranet servers without dependency on public cloud APIs or external telemetry servers.

Clause 20: Ministry of Home Affairs Cyber Compliance

System architecture adheres to Indian Government Cyber Security Guidelines (CERT-In) and defence data sovereignty mandates.

Clause 21: Anti-Reidentification Protections

Aggregated battalion metrics shown in officer dashboards employ minimum cohort thresholds ($k \ge 5$) to prevent re-identifying individual personnel.

Clause 22: Secure Inter-Service Communication

Communication between the Node.js web portal and the FastAPI Python ML service is strictly bound to localhost loopback with input schema validation.

Clause 23: Absolute Prohibition on Third-Party Data Sharing

No personnel welfare data is ever shared with, sold to, or processed by external third parties, commercial advertisers, or unaccredited entities.

Clause 24: Immediate Emergency Escalation Protocols

In scenarios of imminent severe crisis, the system provides prominent direct links to toll-free 24/7 human crisis lines (`1800-180-4024`) bypassing automated processing.

Clause 25: Security Vulnerability Disclosure & Patching

Continuous vulnerability assessments, automated test suites (`test_e2e.js`), and dependency reviews are maintained to ensure rapid threat remediation.

Clause 26: Independent Welfare Ethics Oversight

The platform is governed under the oversight of an independent Armed Forces Welfare & Ethics Board tasked with periodic algorithmic reviews and personnel advocacy.
