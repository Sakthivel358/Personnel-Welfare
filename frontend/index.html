Engineered for High-Stress Operational Welfare

Providing holistic, non-punitive welfare tracking that respects personnel privacy while enabling institutional support.

🎯

Trained Machine Learning Model

Built with Python, FastAPI, and Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classifier trained on documented synthetic operational datasets. Generates objective concern probabilities without hardcoded rule formulas.

📊

Why This Result? & What Changed?

Transparent explainability detailing model-derived contributing indicators and comparing recent check-in differences across rest, duty hours, and pressure without claiming single-factor causation.

🛡️

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

AI does not make automated administrative or disciplinary decisions. Elevated signals route to authorized Welfare Officers for confidential human review, check-in validation, and care coordination.

📈

Longitudinal Welfare Trajectory

Interactive dynamic Chart.js time-series charts visualizing stress trajectories across 7D, 30D, 90D, and 6M with markers for follow-up sessions and support interventions.

🔄

Continuous Recovery Journey

Structured 6-stage lifecycle tracking from early concern identification through supportive recommendations, officer review, follow-up check-in, and trajectory re-analysis.

🔒

Zero-Leakage Security & RBAC

Salted Bcrypt password hashing (10 rounds), HttpOnly secure cookies, database session persistence across restarts, and server-side Role-Based Access Control.

Explainable AI (XAI)

No Black-Box Predictions

Uniformed personnel and welfare officers need to know why a welfare concern signal was generated. Our platform maps model feature weights directly to operational variables, pinpointing primary drivers such as sleep disruption or continuous shift duty.

"Model-derived contributing indicators represent statistical variance relative to operational baselines, not clinical causation or diagnostic certainty."
