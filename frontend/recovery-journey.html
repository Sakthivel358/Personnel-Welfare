ℹ️ Understanding the 6-Stage Welfare Lifecycle

Operational Protocol

In operational armed forces deployment, when a stress signal is flagged (Stages 1 & 2), the Human-in-the-Loop Officer Review (Stage 4) requires the Unit Welfare Officer to confidentially acknowledge the report before assigning a follow-up consultation (Stage 5). This prevents automated disciplinary conclusions.
