Ethical AI Framework, Data Privacy & Governance Charter

Official Ethical Governance, Data Security, and Operational Privacy Safeguards for WelfareAI.

CRITICAL ETHICAL DIRECTIVE: The system is a WELFARE SUPPORT and DECISION-SUPPORT platform for uniformed forces. It is strictly NOT a medical diagnosis system and must NOT diagnose psychiatric illness. It must NOT be used for promotion, demotion, termination, disciplinary action, performance reviews, or clandestine surveillance.

I. Non-Clinical Boundaries & Welfare Mission

Clause 1: Non-Clinical Decision-Support Scope

WelfareAI is engineered solely to compute observational stress strain indices from self-reported operational factors. It does not perform psychiatric diagnostics, prescribe pharmaceutical treatment, or substitute for formal medical evaluation by qualified military medical officers.

Clause 2: Absolute Non-Punitive Doctrine

Check-in scores and AI attribution signals are protected by an inviolable non-punitive doctrine. Data collected in this portal shall under no circumstances be entered into Annual Confidential Reports (ACR), court-martial inquiries, promotion screening, or disciplinary reviews.

II. Data Isolation, Cryptography & Access Governance

Clause 3: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture

Access is strictly compartmentalized across three isolated roles: (a) Personnel: private access to personal records; (b) Welfare Officers: view triage flags and follow-up schedules without command authority; (c) System Admins: infrastructure health and audit oversight with zero access to private questionnaires.

Clause 4: Zero Clandestine Surveillance Policy

The platform contains zero clandestine background tracking, GPS surveillance, keystroke logging, audio snooping, or biometric surveillance.

Clause 5: Cryptographic Password Hashing & Audit Logging

All credentials use Bcrypt with 10 salt rounds. Critical transactions (alert reviews, shift allocations, follow-up scheduling) are recorded in an append-only audit ledger.

III. Explainable AI & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

Clause 6: Algorithmic Transparency & Anti-Black Box Guarantee

Every prediction is accompanied by a transparent mathematical feature attribution breakdown derived from Random Forest tree attributions, showing exact operational contributors.

Clause 7: Human-in-the-Loop Oversight (No Automated Directives)

The AI model cannot autonomously trigger administrative actions, duty transfers, or roster removals. All supportive interventions require human Welfare Officer assessment and mutual consultation.

🩺 Welfare Officer Triage & Review Protocol

Q: What is the recommended workflow when a High Welfare Signal is flagged?

A: 1. Review the signal's top operational drivers (e.g. duty hours, sleep deficit).
2. Click Review Signal to record an officer decision (*Acknowledge, Assign Follow-up, or Monitor*).
3. Schedule a supportive 1-on-1 check-in or use the AI Shift Pacing Optimizer to recommend a 48h rest rotation before their next heavy deployment.

Q: How does the AI model evaluate operational strain?

A: A trained Random Forest Classifier evaluates 8 key operational features against baseline statistical distributions to compute the composite welfare risk index (0–100%) and feature attribution breakdown.

Q: How does follow-up re-analysis work?

A: When assigned personnel submit subsequent check-ins, the system compares the new risk score against their baseline, computing the welfare trajectory delta (*Improved, Stable, or Elevated*) in the Follow-up Workflow tab.
