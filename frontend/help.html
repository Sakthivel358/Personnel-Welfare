WelfareAI Knowledge Base & Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive operational guide covering AI stress modeling, explainability, data privacy, welfare triage, and support workflows for uniformed personnel and officers.

🌐 General

Q: What is WelfareAI and who is it designed for?

A: WelfareAI is an evidence-based, human-in-the-loop decision-support platform engineered specifically for armed forces and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) personnel and welfare officers. It monitors operational strain, workload velocity, and recovery indicators to prevent chronic burnout and promote force resilience.

Q: Is WelfareAI a clinical diagnostic or psychiatric platform?

A: No. WelfareAI explicitly identifies welfare-risk patterns and operational stress strain. It is strictly non-clinical, does not deliver a medical diagnosis, and does not replace certified military medical or psychological personnel.

Q: What is the core non-disciplinary doctrine of the platform?

A: Under the Force Welfare Directive, an ML prediction must never automatically become a disciplinary action. Check-in results and welfare flags are legally and procedurally prohibited from being used in Annual Confidential Reports (ACR), promotion evaluations, or disciplinary inquiries.

👤 Personnel

Q: How do I complete my regular stress check-in?

A: Navigate to the Stress Check-in tab, enter your weekly duty hours, sleep hours, subjective work pressure rating, and the 10-item PSS-10 questionnaire. The process takes less than 3 minutes and immediately computes your personal welfare score.

Q: How frequently should I submit a check-in?

A: Standard recommended frequency is once per week, or immediately following intense field operations, prolonged night shifts, or high-altitude patrols.

Q: Can I view my personal historical baseline and what changed?

A: Yes. The What Changed? view compares Your Normal Pattern vs Current across workload, rest, fatigue, and stress indicators. If you have fewer than 2 check-ins, the system clearly shows: Baseline not established yet.

Q: Can I request welfare support if my score is low or moderate?

A: Yes, absolutely. Personnel can request confidential welfare support at any time via the Welfare Support page, regardless of whether the ML model classified them as high concern or not.

🩺 Welfare Officer

Q: What is the 7-stage Welfare Officer review workflow?

A: The non-punitive review workflow follows a strict 7-stage pathway:
1. Alert → 2. Evidence → 3. Contributors → 4. What Changed → 5. Officer Review → 6. Support Action → 7. Follow-up.

Q: How does the AI Shift Pacing & Rest Rotation Optimizer work?

A: The optimizer analyzes fatigue velocity and consecutive shifts to suggest rest rotations (e.g. 48h decompression cycle or daylight administrative duty) for high-strain watch standers to preempt exhaustion before it compromises health.

Q: How do follow-up re-analysis and trajectory tracking work?

A: When a personnel member submits subsequent check-ins after an officer support consultation, the system re-evaluates their welfare risk index against the initial assessment, categorizing the observed change as Improved, Stable, or Elevated.

⚙️ Admin

Q: What system metrics and infrastructure health can administrators monitor?

A: Administrators monitor live Node.js/Express API status, MongoDB database connectivity, FastAPI ML microservice health, and authentic datastore volume counts (users, personnel, check-ins, predictions, alerts, support requests, follow-ups).

Q: How are user accounts and roles governed?

A: Administrators govern role-based access control (RBAC) across three distinct roles: `PERSONNEL`, `WELFARE_OFFICER`, and `ADMIN`. Admins have zero access to private individual questionnaire responses.

Q: How are immutable security audit logs maintained?

A: All authentication events, alert reviews, pacing approvals, and password changes are recorded into an append-only audit collection containing timestamps, personnel IDs, target resources, and client IP addresses.

🤖 ML (Machine Learning)

Q: What machine learning model is utilized and what is its architecture?

A: The platform utilizes a Python Scikit-Learn RandomForestClassifier with 60 ensemble estimators, balanced class weights, and maximum tree depth of 8, served asynchronously via a FastAPI microservice on port 8000.

Q: How is ML explainability and feature attribution computed?

A: Every prediction extracts individual decision path weights and feature attribution percentages from the ensemble trees, showing understandable contributing indicators (e.g. Weekly duty hours > 60h contributed +32% to risk score).

Q: How are the models evaluated (train/test validation)?

A: Models are evaluated using holdout 80/20 train/test splits with verified multi-class metrics including overall accuracy, macro-precision, macro-recall, macro-F1, and full confusion matrices without artificial inflation.

Q: What is the synthetic dataset transparency policy?

A: Synthetic training data is clearly labeled as Synthetic Prototype Training Data. Prototype accuracy is explicitly distinguished from real-world field-validated accuracy.

⌚ Wearables

Q: What wearable biometric telemetry does WelfareAI support?

A: The system ingests Heart Rate Variability (HRV rmssd in ms), Resting Heart Rate (RHR in bpm), daily step counts, and deep sleep recovery hours from authorized defense-grade smart bands.

Q: Is continuous wearable monitoring mandatory or voluntary?

A: Completely voluntary. Personnel can choose whether to link wearable telemetry. When wearable data is absent, the system functions seamlessly using authorized operational and self-check data.

Q: How does wearable data correlate with subjective check-ins?

A: Wearable telemetry provides objective physiological confirmation (e.g. low HRV indicating autonomic sympathetic overdrive) that strengthens the evidence basis of self-reported strain.

📋 PSS-10

Q: What is the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10)?

A: The PSS-10 is the globally recognized 10-item psychometric instrument developed by Dr. Sheldon Cohen to measure the degree to which situations in a person's life are appraised as unpredictable, uncontrollable, and overwhelming.

Q: Which items in the PSS-10 are reverse-scored and why?

A: Items 4, 5, 7, and 8 are positively worded (e.g., feeling confident about handling personal problems) and are reverse-scored (0 → 4, 1 → 3, 2 → 2, 3 → 1, 4 → 0) to ensure psychometric validity.

Q: What do the PSS-10 total scores indicate?

A: Scores range from 0 to 40:
• 0–13: Low perceived operational stress.
• 14–26: Moderate perceived stress (routine coping and pacing recommended).
• 27–40: High perceived stress (proactive rest and welfare check-in recommended).

🛡️ Privacy

Q: How are personal identities separated from welfare analytics?

A: Under Task 36 privacy protection, personal identity information (name, service ID, contact number) is isolated from analytical telemetry using cryptographic pseudonymization, coarse age bucketing, and regional unit clustering.

Q: What is the Privacy Sandbox?

A: The Privacy Sandbox (`privacy-sandbox.html`) demonstrates the exact before & after transformation:
Name: Ravi Kumar / Service ID: CRPF10452 / Age: 27 → User Token: USR_7F29A / Age Group: 25–30 / Unit Group / Workload / Stress Index.

Q: What data masking is applied?

A: Service identifiers are masked in officer triage views (e.g. `CRPF-***52`) and phone numbers are masked (`******4024`) to enforce data minimization.

🔐 Security

Q: How are credentials and sessions secured?

A: Passwords are encrypted using Bcrypt with 10 salt rounds. Sessions use signed HttpOnly JWT cookies with Strict SameSite protection, preventing XSS and CSRF token extraction.

Q: Why is there a two-stage confirmation screen for Change Password?

A: In accordance with Task 40 security requirements, the password-change inputs are hidden behind a secure re-authentication gate to prevent shoulder-surfing and unauthorized password changes on unattended military workstations.

📡 Offline mode

Q: How does WelfareAI operate in remote border posts without internet?

A: The client application stores submitted check-ins locally in an encrypted IndexedDB / LocalStorage queue when network connectivity is lost.

Q: How and when are offline records synchronized?

A: As soon as a network connection or base intranet link is detected, the browser automatically batches queued check-ins to `POST /api/v1/checkin/sync`, preserving original submission timestamps.

💾 Data

Q: What operational and physiological data streams are collected?

A: Authorized data streams include: DUTY (shift length, night watch), WORKLOAD (weekly duty hours), REST_RECOVERY (sleep duration), SELF_CHECK (PSS-10, subjective pressure), and optional WEARABLE (HRV, RHR).

Q: Can personnel export their complete longitudinal welfare data?

A: Yes. Personnel can export their complete check-in history as JSON or CSV files directly from the Settings and Longitudinal Trajectory pages.

🚨 Alerts

Q: How are evidence-based alerts generated?

A: Alerts are generated exclusively when verified data streams cross validated strain thresholds (e.g. cumulative duty > 68h/wk, sleep < 5h, or PSS-10 > 28). Every alert provides a structured explanation (`whyAlertGenerated`) listing the exact triggering factors.

Q: What does 'EVIDENCE — INSUFFICIENT' mean when data is sparse?

A: In accordance with Task 25, if fewer than 2 authorized data streams exist, the system will NOT display High/Moderate/Low simply to fill the screen. It displays WELFARE CONCERN — UNDETERMINED and EVIDENCE — INSUFFICIENT.

Q: Why does every alert explicitly declare non-disciplinary protection?

A: To prevent administrative misuse. Alerts are strictly supportive triage signals for fatigue mitigation, restorative downtime, and voluntary counseling liaison.

🤝 Welfare Support

Q: What voluntary support options are available in the Welfare Support portal?

A: Support options include: 1. Rest & Sleep Restoration (48–72h downtime), 2. Peer Mentorship Connect, 3. Duty Pacing & Shift Adjustment, 4. Confidential Counseling Liaison, and 5. 24/7 Tele-Counseling Helplines (1800-180-4024).

Q: What is the status tracking lifecycle for support requests?

A: Requests progress through 4 clear states: SUBMITTED → UNDER_OFFICER_REVIEW → ACTION_DISPATCHED → RESOLVED, with timestamped notes visible to the personnel member.

⚖️ Ethics

Q: What is the core Ethical AI Charter governing WelfareAI?

A: WelfareAI is founded on three ethical pillars: 1. Absolute Non-Punitive Doctrine, 2. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight (no autonomous administrative actions), and 3. Zero Clandestine Surveillance (no GPS tracking, keylogging, or microphone access).

Q: Can machine learning predictions replace human judgment?

A: Never. The AI model functions solely as an observational decision-support tool. All care interventions, schedule adjustments, and welfare consults require empathetic human officer review.
