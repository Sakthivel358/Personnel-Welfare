Privacy Protection Architecture (Task 36)

Four pillars enforcing identity separation from welfare analytics
Active Enforcement
🔑 1. Cryptographic Pseudonymization

Direct identifiers (Name, Service ID) are converted via one-way HMAC-SHA256 salt hashing into deterministic User Tokens (e.g. USR_7F29A). Analytical ML pipelines never store or process raw military registration numbers.

📊 2. Data Minimization & K-Anonymity

Exact biological age is bucketed into 5-year cohorts (e.g. 25–30) and granular unit postings are coarsened into regional tactical groups. Unnecessary metadata (GPS coordinates, home addresses) is stripped at the ingest gateway.

🛡️ 3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Commanding officers see only aggregated battalion trends ($k \ge 5$). Unit Welfare Officers only access identifiable records during active, voluntary welfare reviews with immutable audit logging (PII_ACCESS_DE_PSEUDONYMIZED).

🎭 4. Appropriate Masking

Service IDs are masked in operational triage rosters (e.g. CRPF*****52), phone numbers are redacted (e.g. ******3210), and names are concealed in default triage views to prevent subconscious bias.
