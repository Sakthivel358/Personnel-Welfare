🎯 Executive Overview

WelfareAI is an end-to-end, privacy-preserving AI decision-support platform designed specifically for uniformed armed forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, and Armed Forces). The system predicts cumulative stress velocity, decomposes operational risk drivers with 8-factor explainable machine learning, and recommends tactical relief pacing before acute burnout occurs.

1. Entry & Authentication Modules

🌐 Landing Page (index.html) Public

What it is: The public mission portal explaining the platform's purpose, national defense compliance, and core capabilities.

  • Role Portals: Direct 1-click access routes for Personnel (Soldiers), Welfare Officers (Adjutants), and System Admins.
  • Interactive Architecture Carousel: Explains the 3-tier pipeline (Data Ingestion → Random Forest Inference → Officer Decision Support).
  • Ethical Highlights: Explains zero-penalty governance to eliminate reporting stigma among Jawans.
🔐 Authentication & Registration (login.html & register.html) Security Core

What it is: Secure entry gateways enforcing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and bcrypt password hashing.

  • Registration: Allows any new personnel to register with Service ID, Rank, Unit, and encrypted credentials.
  • 1-Click Demo Switcher: Built-in instant login buttons for Judges (Personnel, Welfare Officer, Admin) for rapid evaluation without typing.
  • Session Persistence: Signs 7-day cryptographically verified JWT tokens stored in secure local state.

2. Personnel Portal (Soldier View)

📊 Personnel Dashboard (dashboard.html) Personnel Core

What it is: The personal mission control center for the individual soldier.

  • Active Stress Index Card: Displays real-time risk classification (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH) and composite risk percentage.
  • Top Contributing Drivers: Highlights top operational stressors (e.g. 54h watch duty, sleep deficit).
  • Longitudinal Trend Canvas: Visualizes historical risk trajectory over consecutive check-in cycles.
  • Tactical 4-4-4-4 Box-Breathing Decompression Widget: Interactive animated military relaxation tool with 4-second Inhale-Hold-Exhale-Hold cadence to lower heart rate.
📝 Stress Check-in & Wearable Sync (stress-checkin.html) Data Ingestion

What it is: A 3-step structured inventory combining clinical psychometrics with field operational metrics.

  • Tactical Wearable Telemetry Sync: Dual-mode IoT integration (Web Bluetooth BLE scanner + simulated Defense Smart Band telemetry) auto-filling Resting Heart Rate (58 bpm), HRV (68 ms), and Sleep (7.5h).
  • Standardized PSS-10 Inventory: 10 psychometric stress assessment questions.
  • Duty Indicators: Sliders for weekly duty hours, operational pressure, continuous shift days, and social support.
🌲 AI Stress Analysis & Explainability (ai-analysis.html & why-risk-high.html) Explainable ML

What it is: Transparent glass-box explanation decomposing exactly why the AI model predicted the soldier's score.

  • 8-Factor Attribution Decomposition: Interactive bar charts showing individual percentage weights for Watch Hours, Sleep, Work Pressure, Social Support, etc.
  • Plain-Language Insights: Contextual natural language explanations explaining how each factor impacted the prediction.
💡 Personalized Recommendations (recommendations.html) Actionable Relief

What it is: Tailored welfare action items and evidence-based recovery protocols.

  • Factor-Tailored Action Items: Dynamic checklist items generated based on the soldier's highest stress drivers.
  • Interactive Status Cycling: Soldiers can click action cards to toggle between ● New, ⏳ In Progress, and ✓ Completed with live percentage completion bars.
  • Confidential Helplines: 24/7 tele-counseling (1800-180-4024) and Battalion Peer Support desk.
📈 Longitudinal Trajectory & Recovery Journey (trends.html & recovery-journey.html) Trajectory Analytics

What it is: Time-series tracking comparing historical strain levels across weeks and operational deployments.

  • Trajectory Curve: Interactive Chart.js trend line charting resilience progress.
  • What Changed? Comparison: Side-by-side delta table comparing the latest check-in against the previous submission.
  • Recovery Velocity: Measures statistical improvement after rest rotations and supportive interventions.

3. Welfare Officer Portal (Command & Adjutant View)

🩺 Welfare Officer Command Hub (welfare-officer.html) Adjutant Portal

What it is: Comprehensive triage, decision-support, and shift reallocation workspace for unit commanders and doctors.

  • Human-in-the-Loop Triage Queue: Prioritized alert cards generated whenever a soldier's strain exceeds 70%. Officers enter supportive notes and assign follow-up sessions.
  • ⚡ AI Shift & Roster Pacing Optimizer: Analyzes rising fatigue patterns and automatically suggests optimal duty swaps (e.g. rotating night watch standers to daylight base logistics) with 1-click approval (⚡ Approve Pacing Reallocation).
  • 🌱 Follow-up Tracking Workflow: Schedules and tracks 7-day re-analysis milestones to evaluate recovery velocity.
  • 🇮🇳 1-Click Executive PDF Battalion Dossier: Generates a formal printable MHA/CAPF executive readiness report complete with readiness distribution, factor decompositions, and command directives.
  • 🤝 Support Requests Queue: Confidential inbox to respond directly to personnel support tickets.
