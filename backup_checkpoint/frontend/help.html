Frequently Asked Questions & User Guide

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions regarding AI predictions, confidentiality, and welfare follow-ups.

Q: Where is the AI and how is my risk calculated?

A: When you submit your check-in, your inputs are transmitted to our Python FastAPI microservice where a trained Random Forest Classifier evaluates multi-variable operational patterns against baseline statistical distributions to compute your welfare concern level and feature attribution breakdown.

Q: Can my commanding officer see my private answers or use this for discipline?

A: No. The system is strictly insulated for welfare decision-support. Disciplinary bodies and commanding officers do not have access to individual check-in questionnaires. Only authorized Welfare Officers receive priority alerts to schedule supportive follow-up sessions.

Q: What happens if a High Welfare Concern signal is generated?

A: An automated alert is generated in the Welfare Officer portal. A qualified officer reviews the signal, acknowledges it, and may schedule a confidential follow-up session or suggest rest rotation adjustments. Subsequent check-ins automatically update your recovery trajectory.

Q: Who do I contact in case of immediate distress?

A: Call the 24/7 CRPF Tele-Counseling Support Helpline immediately at 1800-180-4024 (Toll-Free) or connect with your battalion medical liaison officer.
