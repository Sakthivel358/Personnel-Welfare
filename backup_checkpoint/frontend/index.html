Engineered for High-Stress Operational Environments

Providing holistic, non-punitive welfare tracking that respects personnel privacy while enabling institutional support.

🎯

Trained Machine Learning Model

Built with Python, FastAPI, and Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classifier (100 trees) trained on synthetic operational datasets. Generates objective concern probabilities without hardcoded rule formulas.

📊

Transparent Explainability

"Why is my risk high?" transparent breakdown displaying model-derived contributing indicators (sleep deficit, shift continuity, operational workload) relative to baseline distributions.

🛡️

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

AI does not make automated administrative or disciplinary decisions. Elevated signals route to authorized Welfare Officers for confidential human review, check-in validation, and care coordination.

📈

Longitudinal Trend Tracking

Interactive dynamic Chart.js time-series charts visualizing stress trajectories across shifts and deployments, enabling personnel and counselors to monitor recovery velocity over time.

🔒

Zero-Leakage Security & RBAC

Bcrypt password hashing, HttpOnly secure cookies, database session persistence, and server-side Role-Based Access Control protecting sensitive personnel records.

🤝

Confidential Support Pathways

One-click direct connection to 24/7 tele-counseling helplines, battalion peer support networks, and family welfare assistance liaison desks.

Explainable AI (XAI)

No Black-Box Predictions

Uniformed personnel and welfare officers need to know why a welfare concern signal was generated. Our platform maps model feature weights directly to operational variables, pinpointing primary drivers such as sleep disruption or continuous shift duty.

"Model-derived contributing indicators represent statistical variance relative to operational baselines, not clinical causation or diagnostic certainty."
